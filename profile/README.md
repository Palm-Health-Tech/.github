# 🌴 Palm Health Tech

**Technology for direct primary care.**

We build the software and hardware stack behind Palm Health — an AI-powered,
member-first direct primary care practice. No insurance middlemen, no 7-minute
slots. Just doctors, data, and tools that work.

## What we build

- 🩺 **Practice management** — AI-assisted scheduling, charting, and member operations for direct primary care
- 🔐 **Privacy infrastructure** — PHI redaction and HIPAA-first data tooling
- 🖥️ **In-clinic hardware** — kiosk firmware for member check-in and vitals
- 🧠 **Clinical AI stack** — on-premise inference, vector search, and voice AI

## Principles

1. **Own your data.** Health records belong to members, not platforms.
2. **Privacy by default.** PHI never touches a third-party service unredacted.
3. **Small surface, real care.** Technology should disappear so clinicians can practice medicine.
